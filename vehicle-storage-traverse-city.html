Let’s look at why proper Vehicle storage is essential:

1. Protection from Weather

Additionally, ice and snow can cause problems with your car’s engine, battery, and tires. 2. Maintain Vehicle Value

For classic cars, luxury automobiles, or even your everyday vehicle, proper storage is key to maintaining its value. Exposure to the elements over time can significantly reduce a car’s resale value. By storing your car in a clean, secure, and climate-controlled environment, you prevent the type of wear and tear that can decrease its value.

Many neighborhoods and homeowners’ associations in Traverse City have parking restrictions, especially for extra vehicles or those not used regularly. Instead of trying to work around these restrictions, storing your Vehicle at a dedicated facility like Luther’s Large Storage is an easy and stress-free solution.

Preparing Your Car for Storage

Before storing your car at Luther’s Large Storage, it’s essential to take a few steps to prepare your vehicle. This helps ensure that your Vehicle remains in excellent condition while it’s in storage:

  1. Clean Your Car Thoroughly: A clean car is less likely to develop issues during storage. Wash the exterior to remove dirt, road salt, and debris, and vacuum the interior to eliminate crumbs or food particles that could attract pests.

  2. Top Off Fluids: Make sure all fluids, including oil, coolant, and windshield washer fluid, are topped off. Add a fuel stabilizer to prevent fuel from deteriorating while your car is in storage.

  3. Disconnect the Battery: If you’re storing your Vehicle for a long time, disconnect the battery to prevent it from draining. Alternatively, you could use a battery maintainer to keep it charged.

  4. Inflate the Tires: Ensure your tires are properly inflated to avoid flat spots, which can occur when a car remains stationary for an extended period.

  5. Cover Your Vehicle: Even in a climate-controlled unit, it’s a good idea to cover your Vehicle to protect it from dust and potential scratches.
