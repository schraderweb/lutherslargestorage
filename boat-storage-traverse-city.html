Why Boat Storage Is Essential

Boats, like any large investment, require proper care and attention, particularly when they’re not in use. Traverse City experiences a full range of weather conditions, from hot summers to frigid winters, and storing your boat outdoors without protection can lead to costly damage. Here are a few reasons why securing boat storage is so important:

1. Protection from Harsh Weather

Michigan winters are notoriously harsh, with freezing temperatures, snow, ice, and high winds. Storing your boat outdoors during this season can lead to significant damage. Cold weather can cause cracks in your boat’s hull, while ice and snow can weigh down on the structure, potentially leading to leaks or other long-term issues. By using a dedicated storage facility like Luther’s Large Storage, you shield your boat from these harsh elements, extending its lifespan and maintaining its value.

2. Preventing UV Damage

Even during the warmer months, boats are susceptible to damage from UV rays. Prolonged exposure to the sun can fade your boat’s paint, crack vinyl upholstery, and weaken the integrity of certain materials. Storing your boat indoors at Luther’s Large Storage protects it from the harmful effects of UV radiation.

3. Preserving Mechanical Integrity

Boats are complex machines, with various mechanical and electrical systems that need to be kept in good working order. Leaving your boat exposed to the elements can cause moisture buildup, leading to rust and corrosion in vital systems like the engine, wiring, and electronics. By opting for secure, climate-controlled storage, you reduce the risk of moisture damage and preserve the mechanical integrity of your vessel.

4. Security and Peace of Mind

Boat theft and vandalism are unfortunate risks boat owners must consider. Leaving your boat in your driveway or in an unsecured lot increases these risks. Luther’s Large Storage offers a secure, gated facility with 24/7 surveillance, so you can rest easy knowing your boat is protected from unauthorized access and potential damage.

Preparing Your Boat for Storage

Before placing your boat in storage, it’s essential to prepare it properly to ensure it remains in top condition while it’s not in use. Here are a few tips to help you get your boat ready for storage:

  1. Clean the Boat: Thoroughly clean your boat’s exterior and interior to remove dirt, saltwater, and debris. A clean boat is less likely to develop mold or corrosion during storage.

  2. Drain Fluids: Be sure to drain any water from your boat’s plumbing system and check all fuel and oil levels. You may also want to add a fuel stabilizer to the gas tank to prevent condensation.

  3. Protect the Engine: Flush the engine with fresh water and apply fogging oil to prevent rust and corrosion. Disconnect the battery and store it separately in a dry, warm place.

  4. Cover the Boat: Even if storing indoors, use a boat cover to protect against dust, debris, and potential scratches.
